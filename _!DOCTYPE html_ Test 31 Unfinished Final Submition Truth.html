How does Liberation/Defense/Invasion Work?

Liberation

There are 2 ways to liberate a planet.
1. Helldiver intervention. This is the most common way to liberate a planet.
Each mission and operation contributes to Liberation, and every lose works against liberation.

2. Planet siege. This is a rare way of liberation.
This happens when an enemy controlled planet is surrounded by Super Earth controlled planets.
This planet liberates "by itself" and requires no Helldiver intervention. This can be seen on a planet when the enemy resistance is "-1.00"


Defense/Invasion

Defense occurs when a planet is under invasion.
Defense: Planets with a defense campaign lose liberation at an increased rate.
These planets gain the evacuate citizens and high value personnel missions.
Invasion: The invaded planets can be free of invasion and remain under Super Earth control if the defense campaign succeeds
Or if the source planets of the invasion gets liberated. This stops the invasion and liberates the source planet.


How to enter first-person mode

Entering first-person mode increases recoil control and zooms you in on your scope.
Hold down your left trigger to zoom in. Next, press your right joy-stick to enter first-person mode.
Please note that this only works when zooming in. It does not work if you are not actively zooming in.

How to activate your backpacks

Most tac-packs require to be activated (ex. Portable Hellbomb, Supply pack, etc) while some are already activated, mainly the guard dogs.
To utilize your tac-pack you need to press down on the D-pad, with some exceptions like mobility tac-packs, they need to be activated by holding down the "X" button if you're on Playstation, and the "A"button if you're on Xbox or Steam Deck.
Other exceptions include the directional shield and ballistic shield which require you to Aim-Down-Sights to use.

How to change live weapons

To change a weapon's settings mid-mission, hold down your reload button, and follow the respective D-pad button to change the specific setting.



Please note that every weapon is different. Some weapons may not have optic changes, firing modes, flashlight levels, or under-barrel weapons.
Some weapons may not have any at all.

Reloading

Every weapon in the game has a reload.
For energy/laser weapons, most of them do not need to be reloaded, but there are some exceptions, such as the Las-rifle.
For weapons that come with a backpack, you just hit the reload button.
These weapons have a one in the chamber mechanic so if you lose your pack, you'll still have 1 mag.
There are some exceptions such as the belt feds, where the pack is REQUIRED to fire the weapon. These weapons also DO NOT have a one in the chamber mechanic.
Many support weapons have a stationary reload, so be careful where and when you reload.
!IMPORTANT!: When you reload, all left over ammunition that is still inside the magazine is discarded unless the weapon has the "rounds reload" trait.
